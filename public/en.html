Fair questions

Frequently asked questions

Can Tuijo read our messages?

No. Messages are end-to-end encrypted with RSA-2048 and AES-256. Private keys stay on your device and never reach our servers. Only encrypted data travels through the cloud, unreadable to anyone but you and your partner.

How do my partner and I connect?

With a simple QR code. One phone shows the code, the other scans it, and they exchange only public keys. In seconds the two devices are securely linked.

Is shared location really private?

Yes. GPS coordinates are encrypted with an AES-256 key dedicated to the session. No plaintext location is ever stored on the server, and sharing stops on its own at the limit you choose (1 or 8 hours).

Which devices does it work on?

Tuijo is available on iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play), with an interface in Italian, English, Spanish and Catalan.

How much does it cost?

Download Tuijo from the stores and start your private conversation right away. Check the store pages for up-to-date details on availability and pricing.
