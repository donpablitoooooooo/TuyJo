TuyJo v1.34.0

NEW:
🔐 Certificate backup: you choose how to keep it — automatic cloud (Google/iCloud) to get everything back when you change phone, or manual with copy to clipboard (no cloud, full control)
♻️ New Restore page: it finds the certificate by itself (on the phone or in the cloud) and brings you back into your chat without re-scanning the QR
🗑️ Delete Messages redesigned: wipe only this phone, only your partner's phone, or everything (server included). Whoever stays loses nothing, and selective wipes are recovered by pairing again
📅 Calendar: new "Add" button and alerts up to 1 week before (1h, 2h, 8h, 1 day, 2 days, 1 week)
🌍 Complete translations in Italian, English, Spanish and Catalan

BUG FIXES:
⌨️ The keyboard no longer closes while your partner is typing
📍 Fixed location sharing opening at half screen
🔕 A wiped phone no longer receives notifications for the old chat

NOTES:
📱 Compatibility: Android 6.0+ and iOS 15.0+
🔐 End-to-end encryption guaranteed
